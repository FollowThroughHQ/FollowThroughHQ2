# Business plan and validation

## The idea
Many new service businesses (HVAC, roofing, detailing, cleaning, etc.) do good work but lose jobs because they are not trained in sales. The owner has sales skills and access to a list of recently opened businesses. The goal is to help them sell better and, later, get paid for it.

## Honest assessment
- A generic PDF of tips is easy to copy and free elsewhere, so it is a hook, not the product.
- Free plus ads is weak: ads pay little per visitor and the owner's strength is talking to people, not building traffic.
- New businesses often lack leads before they lack closing skill. The pitch should be "turn the inquiries you get into booked jobs."

## Offer ladder
1. **Free lead magnet:** a one-page, trade-specific playbook ("7 objections roofers hear and what to say").
2. **Low-priced playbook:** scripts, follow-up templates and a quote checklist, about $29-49.
3. **Personalized help:** custom scripts or a call review, about $200-500.
4. **Done-for-you follow-up:** written follow-up on quotes, paid per booked job or commission. Best fit while working from Europe because it is asynchronous.

## Visibility Autopilot (monthly service)
New businesses often lack leads before they lack closing skill, so alongside the ladder there is a monthly service that helps them get found: listings on 40+ directories, Google Business Profile care and website fixes, on one bill, with results in plain numbers.
- **Starter, $49/month:** a monthly website check with fixes ranked, a listing check and a report. Done by hand with free tools, so it costs nothing to start.
- **Growth, $199/month:** listings and the Google profile looked after for them.
- **Done-for-you, from $499/month:** everything, plus the website fixes and a monthly strategy call.
- Month to month, no setup fee, free cancellation.
- It leads naturally into the sales offers: more calls from being found, then more of them won with the scripts.

How it runs and what's left to launch: `10-visibility-autopilot.md`.

Later income options: affiliate links for quoting and scheduling software (Jobber, Housecall Pro, etc.), once there is an audience that trusts the site.

## Two-week validation plan (about $0-50)
- **Days 1-3:** pick one high-ticket trade (roofing or HVAC), write the one-page asset, build a Google Sheet of 50 target businesses (name, contact method, source).
- **Days 4-10:** send personalized messages mentioning something specific about each business, then one follow-up after 3-4 days. Offer the one-pager free for a reply.
- **Days 10-14:** offer a 15-minute call to people who engage. Ask what happens to their leads and what they do after sending a quote.
- **Track:** reply rate, call acceptance, and the exact words they use for their problem (use these in sales copy).
- **Test willingness to pay:** after 5-10 engaged people, offer the paid version or a custom-script session at a set price. 2-3 yeses is a signal.

## Working from Europe with US customers
- Lean on async channels (email, Facebook and Instagram messages, Google Business messages, contact forms).
- Be upfront about being in Europe. Lead with what you do.
- A US virtual number (OpenPhone or similar, about $15/month) makes calls easy. Schedule calls in their early morning or after 4 pm (your evening).
- Take payments with Stripe, PayPal or a merchant-of-record platform (Lemon Squeezy, Gumroad), which can handle sales tax and VAT. Check availability in your country.

## Outreach rules (not legal advice)
- **Email (CAN-SPAM):** B2B cold email is allowed with a real sender identity, a physical mailing address and a working opt-out honored promptly. Use a separate domain for outreach.
- **Text:** avoid cold texting (TCPA).
- **Calls:** B2B cold calling is less restricted, but avoid autodialers and robocalls.
- **GDPR:** as an EU-based sender you may still need to handle contact data carefully. Keep it minimal and delete on request.
- Check with a lawyer before scaling.

## Metrics
Messages sent, reply rate, call rate, pain points mentioned, paid conversions, playbook signups per trade. For Visibility Autopilot: inquiries per plan, paying customers per plan, cancellations, and hours spent per customer each month.
