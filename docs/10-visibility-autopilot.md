# Visibility Autopilot: how it runs and what's left to launch

The paid service on `/visibility`: directory listings, Google Business Profile care and website fixes for local service businesses, on one monthly bill. Its rules for customers are on `/service-terms`. Why it exists and how it's pitched are in `09-value-propositions.md`; where it sits among the offers is in `01-business-plan.md`.

## The plans

| Plan | Price | What's included |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $49/month | A monthly website check with the fixes ranked, a listing check across 40+ directories, a monthly report. The owner makes the fixes. |
| Growth | $199/month | Starter, plus listings submitted and managed, and the Google profile looked after: weekly posts, review replies, photo updates, new-review alerts. |
| Done-for-you | from $499/month | Growth, plus the website fixes made for them and a monthly strategy call. The price is agreed in writing before they start. |

Every plan is month to month: no setup fee, no contract, free cancellation by email. The month already paid for runs to its end and isn't refunded.

## How Starter works without software

No tool to build or rent: the checks are done by hand with free tools and three shared Google Sheets, and the customer gets the results by email. A tool can come later, once there are customers to pay for it.

1. **Website check (monthly).**
   - Speed and basic search problems: run the site through Google PageSpeed Insights (free, pagespeed.web.dev), on mobile.
   - Search problems Google sees: ask the owner to add you to Google Search Console (free) for their site.
   - What stops visitors from calling, checked by hand on a phone: the phone number is tappable at the top, the services and service area are clear, the quote form works, reviews are shown, and license and insurance are mentioned where the state requires it.
   - Write each problem in the website check sheet, marked high, medium or low, with one plain sentence on how to fix it. Send the top five.
2. **Listing check.** Search each directory on the list for the business name and phone, and mark it correct, wrong or missing in the listing check sheet. Check all 40+ in the first month and every three months after that, and the ten that matter most every month.
3. **Monthly report.** One page from the report template: Google profile calls, website clicks and direction requests (from the profile's Performance page, if the owner adds you as a manager or sends a screenshot), new reviews and average rating, listings correct out of the total, website speed and problems fixed. Each number next to last month's.

Cost: $0 in tools. Time it on the first customer: a guess is 2–3 hours for the first month and about an hour a month after that.

2. Make the three Google Sheets: the website check, the listing check and the monthly report template.
