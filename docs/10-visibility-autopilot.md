# Visibility Autopilot: the plans and what's left to launch

The paid service on `/visibility`: directory listings, Google Business Profile care and website fixes for local service businesses, on one monthly bill. Its rules for customers are on `/service-terms`. Why it exists and how it's pitched are in `09-value-propositions.md`; where it sits among the offers is in `01-business-plan.md`.

## The plans

| Plan | Price | What's included |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $49/month | A monthly website check with the fixes ranked, a listing check across 40+ directories, a monthly report. The owner makes the fixes. |
| Growth | $199/month | Starter, plus listings submitted and managed, and the Google profile looked after: weekly posts, review replies, photo updates, new-review alerts. |
| Done-for-you | from $499/month | Growth, plus the website fixes made for them and a monthly strategy call. The price is agreed in writing before they start. |

Every plan is month to month: no setup fee, no contract, free cancellation by email. The month already paid for runs to its end and isn't refunded.

## How each plan works

Step by step, in `01-business-plan.md` (onboarding, then Starter, Growth and Done-for-you). Everything runs by hand with free tools and three Google Sheets: the website check, the listing check and the monthly report.

## To do before selling

1. Write the list of 40+ directories for local US service businesses, and check each is still running and free to list on. The site promises 40+, so the list has to hold at least that many.
2. Make the three Google Sheets: the website check, the listing check and the monthly report template.
3. Choose a payment provider (Stripe, or Lemon Squeezy, which also handles sales tax) and check it works from your country. Set up monthly subscriptions for $49 and $199, and a way to bill a custom Done-for-you price. Then add the provider's name to the Privacy page and the service terms.
4. Check whether Google still lets businesses answer questions (Q&A) on their profile. It was left off the page because Google seemed to be switching it off in late 2025.
5. Have a lawyer read the service terms and the Privacy page's Visibility Autopilot section (see `04-legal-checklist.md`), and ask an accountant how to invoice US customers and whether VAT applies.
6. Keep a simple customer list (a Google Sheet): business, plan, start date, accesses granted, cancellation date.
7. Ask Emil to put the changes live.
